Callbacks & Warranty — Branch Drill-Through

2026 YTD through Sept 30

How to read this. HVAC non-revenue recalls (Callback + Warranty) with no revenue on the recall — revenue-generating recall jobs are excluded — completed Jan 1 to Sep 30, 2026. Pick a branch, then stack filters: cause, specific issue, source type, and source age. Everything, including MQ Check-out, recomputes for what's in view.

Recall rate (KPIs). Install = the share of 2026 installs that generated at least one recall (distinct recalled installs ÷ 2026 installs). Install = system replacements (sold star SKU) plus duct, insulation, mini-split and partial installs. Service = Demand/Maintenance plus H-Install Parts and Clean/Seal/IAQ jobs — part-swaps behave like repairs, not replacements. Each job counts once even if it came back more than once. Branch-level; these KPIs do not change with the cause/source filters, and being year-to-date they undercount later-in-the-year installs.

Cause & issue. From Recall Issue Type (Labor / Equipment / Both). Tap a cause, then a specific issue to drill. No-Fault callbacks within 120 days of the source were reclassed to Workmanship.

Source & age. Each recall is traced to its source job. Source age = days from that job to the recall. On the chart, the Service bar is split into technician (Demand/Maintenance source) and installer (Install Parts / non-star install source) so you can see how much service volume is really part-swap work. Tap a chart bar to filter to that source + age.

MQ Check-out. Share of the linked recalls in view whose source job has an MQ vitals (check-out) score on file. Unlinked recalls have no source, so they're excluded from this rate.

Star rating tab. Counts installed systems (not jobs), so a job that drops two systems counts twice and a job with two different star tiers is split across them. 2026 star systems = HVAC Install jobs completed Jan 1–Sep 20 in active branches, exploded to systems: each full-system SKU is one system at its star; a matched outdoor + indoor pair (AC/condenser + furnace/air handler) is one system at the higher of the two stars; leftover single components and condenser + coil swaps are Partial. A system is recalled if a linked recall is attributed to it (two-tier linking). Each recall is attributed to one system on its source job: a home with two systems and a single callback counts one recalled system, and only a second, separate callback flags the second — so one recall is never counted twice. Rate = recalled ÷ installed systems. Each recalled system carries the cause and age band of its callback (earliest first), so Cause and Age partition the recalled total rather than overlapping — Labor + Equipment + Both falls short of All only by the Not-classified systems, and each system sits in exactly one age band. The Branch filter narrows to one branch (default Company = all branches); tiers under 20 systems are dropped from the high/low call-out. System total ties the RvR mix (3,769 vs RvR 3,761); 3★ is the tier where the tiering rule differs most.
